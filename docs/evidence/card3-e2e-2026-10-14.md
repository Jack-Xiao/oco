## Product defect found by c14 and fixed
Symptom: one `draft.insert` was posted twice (draftAcks 2, header twice in the composer, 664 vs 332 chars). Cause: `PanelRegistry.insertDraft` calls `panel.reveal()` (fires onDidChangeViewState, which calls `flushDrafts`) and then `flushDrafts` itself; the page appends on every insert and does not de-duplicate by id. Fix (apps/extension/src/panels.ts): `flushDrafts` skips ids already posted and not yet acked (`sentDrafts` WeakMap; cleared on the page's `ready` and on `draft.ack`). After the fix c14 and c15 both show exactly one insert (acks +1, header once). This affects every insertDraft caller (selection, problem, terminal, review notes, fix).
